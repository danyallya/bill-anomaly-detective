# Product brief

Problem: Compare submitted bills and explain what changed.

Audience: households and small businesses

## First release

One user role, one core record (bill-periods) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Separate observed differences from estimated causes; never invent tariff terms.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
